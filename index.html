Comprehensive answers to questions about TRON AML compliance, TRC-20 risk scoring, USDT transaction tracing, crypto KYC, and blockchain due diligence.

What Is GetBlockAML?

GetBlockAML is a professional blockchain AML compliance platform for the TRON network. It provides instant risk scores for any TRC-20 wallet address, traces USDT transaction flows across multiple hops, screens against global sanctions databases including OFAC and UN, detects mixer and dark web exposure, and produces downloadable PDF compliance reports accepted by exchanges and regulators.

How Do I Run an AML Check on a TRON Address?

Connect your TRON wallet (TronLink or TokenPocket), enter the address you want to screen in the AML Scan field, confirm the 0.01 USDT scan fee payment, and receive your complete risk report in under 2 seconds. What Risk Indicators Does GetBlockAML Check?

GetBlockAML evaluates 40+ AML risk signals including: direct sanctions list matches (OFAC, UN, EU, HM Treasury), indirect exposure to sanctioned entities, mixer and tumbler usage, dark web marketplace fund flows, ransomware payment address links, exchange withdrawal pattern anomalies, layering behaviors, DEX wash trading, and source-of-funds origin analysis across 5 transaction hops.

How Is the 0–100 AML Risk Score Calculated?

GetBlockAML's risk score is a weighted composite of all 40+ risk signals. Sanctions matches carry the highest weight, followed by direct mixer usage, dark web exposure, and ransomware links. Medium-weight signals include indirect exposure (funds that passed through risky wallets 2–5 hops away) and behavioral anomalies. The final 0–100 score maps to Low (0–30), Medium (31–69), and High (70–100) risk tiers.

Why Is TRON AML Compliance Important?

TRON hosts over $60B of Tether USDT — the world's largest stablecoin ecosystem by network. Its low fees and high throughput make it the preferred network for global crypto P2P trading, OTC deals, and remittances. The same properties also attract illicit use. Exchanges, OTC desks, and payment processors increasingly require AML clearance for TRON USDT transactions to satisfy regulatory obligations and avoid frozen funds.

What Sanctions Databases Does GetBlockAML Screen Against?

GetBlockAML screens against four major global sanctions regimes in real time: the OFAC SDN (Specially Designated Nationals) list published by the US Treasury, the UN Security Council Consolidated Sanctions List, the EU Consolidated Financial Sanctions Database, and the HM Treasury UK Sanctions List. Combined, these cover 15,000+ designated individuals, entities, and crypto wallet addresses.

What Happens If a TRON Address Scores High Risk?

A score of 70 or above indicates significant AML risk. GetBlockAML's report will identify the specific risk factors — whether sanctions match, mixer exposure, dark web link, or other flags. Recommended actions include: avoiding transactions with the flagged address, filing a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) if required by your jurisdiction, contacting the relevant platform compliance team, and retaining the GetBlockAML PDF as documented due diligence evidence.

What Is Mixer Detection in Crypto AML?

Mixer detection identifies TRON wallet addresses that have directly interacted with mixing services — protocols designed to obscure the transaction trail of cryptocurrency by pooling and re-distributing funds from multiple sources. GetBlockAML detects both direct mixer usage (the address sent funds to a known TRON mixer) and indirect exposure (the address received funds that previously passed through a mixer), applying different risk weights to each.

How Does Source-of-Funds Analysis Work?

GetBlockAML traces the origin of cryptocurrency in a wallet by following the transaction history backwards for up to 5 hops. It calculates what percentage of funds originated from clean sources (regulated exchanges, verified P2P platforms), medium-risk sources (unverified P2P, DEX swaps), and high-risk sources (mixers, dark web, sanctioned entities). The result is a fund-origin breakdown expressed as clean/medium/high risk percentages.

What Is the Difference Between AML Scan and View AML?

AML Scan initiates a fresh, real-time check of a TRON address — all 40+ risk signals are re-evaluated against current sanctions databases and live on-chain data. This costs 0.01 USDT. View AML retrieves a previously generated scan result for an address without incurring a new scan fee — useful for reviewing a report you've already generated, or accessing a cached scan within its 24-hour validity window.

What Is KYC/AML Compliance for Crypto in 2025?

KYC (Know Your Customer) and AML (Anti-Money Laundering) compliance in crypto refers to the regulatory obligation of Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs) to verify customer identities and monitor transactions for illicit activity. In 2025, major regulators globally (FATF, EU MiCA, FinCEN) require crypto businesses to screen wallet addresses and transaction histories, maintain records, and report suspicious activity — making tools like GetBlockAML essential for legal operation.

Scan results always reflect the most current available data.

What Is the FATF Travel Rule and How Does GetBlockAML Support It?

FATF Recommendation 16 (the Travel Rule) requires Virtual Asset Service Providers to collect and transmit originator and beneficiary information for crypto transfers above certain thresholds. GetBlockAML's risk scoring, counterparty identification, and transaction graph analysis support Travel Rule compliance by helping VASPs assess counterparty risk before transfers and document due diligence. GetBlockAML reports are structured to align with FATF compliance documentation standards.

Is GetBlockAML Compliant with EU MiCA Regulations?

GetBlockAML's AML risk scoring and PDF compliance reports are designed to support EU MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation) compliance requirements for CASPs (Crypto-Asset Service Providers). MiCA's AML obligations, effective from 2024–2025, require CASPs to perform risk assessments on crypto transactions — GetBlockAML provides the on-chain risk data and documentation needed to satisfy these requirements for TRON-based assets.

Yes. GetBlockAML's transaction graph analysis identifies wash trading signatures: circular fund flows between related wallets, abnormal transaction frequency patterns, DEX volume generation without corresponding external fund flows, and price manipulation behaviors on TRON-based DEXes. Wash trading exposure is flagged as a medium-to-high risk indicator, as it frequently co-occurs with market manipulation, pump-and-dump schemes, and fraud.

How Long Are GetBlockAML Scan Results Valid?

Fresh AML Scan results are cached for 24 hours — within this window, View AML retrieves the result without a new scan fee.
